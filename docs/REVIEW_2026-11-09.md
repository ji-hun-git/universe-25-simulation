# Review 2026-11-09 — environment and methods (five independent read-only reviews)

Requested by Jason: "review all the environment and methods to make this the best testing bed for
anything (make the best replica of universe25)".
- Five reviewers worked in parallel: fidelity to the record, simulation methods, calibration
  and statistics, the learning and measurement substrate, and engineering.
- None changed the repo; each ran only short single-process checks.
- This page merges their findings, separates what is safe to change from what reopens the frozen
  calibration, and orders the work. The NOTES entries of 2026-11-09 give the details of the two
  defects already fixed.

## Bottom line

- **What is strong.** Research hygiene is unusually good: preregistration, separate random
  streams, bit-identity checks, H2 audits and honest NOTES. The cited record numbers check out
  against the 1973 paper, decoded page by page.
- **What the replica does and doesn't capture.** The model reproduces Calhoun's population curve
  rather than his system. Three structural choices carry the fit:
  - a playground Calhoun never had (with it off, the curve shifts: N@315 890, peak 2431 at day
    449);
  - a 4 × 4 floor grid instead of 16 wall cells around an open centre;
  - a calendar gate for phase A, which forces compensating errors later (phase B and C double
    too fast, every seed).
- **The calibration.** It is one point on a ridge: about 35 searched and 12 hand-set
  parameters against about 6 informative landmarks from one colony. It carries no uncertainty
  forward. The 3-seed guard is weak in both directions.
- **The play programme.** Two reported results depend on measurement artefacts:
  - criterion 5 counts rows where reflexes forbid any free action, so H-e's only positive
    criterion is partly mechanical;
  - the learner's free-time choices sit below its entropy temperature, so mixing at chance is
    forced by the training setup, not learned.
- **Code safety.** Engineering is a single-track research codebase:
  - no run provenance;
  - golden hashes live only in prose;
  - typos in configs load silently;
  - `backend: auto` silently selects a Warp path that lacks three biology processes.

## Findings by area (major and above)

**Fidelity** (would change calibrated behaviour; reopens calibration):
1. **The playground is inside the calibrated colony.** Cells around the toys hold 43–59 % of the
   animals; with the playground off the curve moves a lot. The playground should be a
   manipulation measured against a playground-free record configuration.
2. **Phase A is a gate** (no conception before day 81, so first litters at 102 are an input).
   - The record: first young counted from day 86 in the 1973 figures, and 50–65 days elsewhere.
   - Growth never slows from a 55-day to a 145-day doubling at day 315.
   - Founders are 60–90 days old (Calhoun: 48).
3. **Pen geometry.** It should be 16 cells along the walls (4 per wall), each with 4 tunnels ×
   4 stacked boxes, a wall hopper for 25 mice and 4 bottles for 8, and a shared open centre.
   Nests are points, and mice are kicked to random boxes anywhere.
4. **No territories or social roles.** "Territorial" means only rank > 0.55, and any male can
   sire. So the model has no brood groups (about 14 at day 315), no young-per-group hierarchy
   (111 … 13) and no role saturation.
5. **Behavioural categories are merged.** Withdrawn males and beautiful ones are one role,
   WITHDRAW drifts to a corner, beautiful ones fight, and there is no maternal aggression or
   eviction of young.
6. **Life history compensates for the gate.** Litters average 7.25 (BALB/c 5–6), and only
   about 33 % of pups are weaned in phase B.
7. **Development** is driven by a colony-wide count, not by the care each animal received.
8. **Peak day** should be scored as plateau onset (Calhoun: growth "abruptly ceased" on day
   560).
9. **Sex.** No sex-biased mortality: male fraction 0.49 at day 1200, while the record runs
   22 M / 100 F at 1444.

**Simulation methods**:
- *Changes calibrated behaviour (flag + adjudication):*
  - The nursing-disturbance term reads a stale per-tick cell-count cache, pre-move on 59 % of
    ticks. This also breaks dt-invariance.
  - Aggression is resolved in slot order.
  - Age accumulates in float32.
- *Safe:*
  - `backend: auto` → Warp on this machine; Warp lacks adoption, the nest kick and the
    maternal-competence rule. Make `auto` pick NumPy.
  - Litters are dropped silently at capacity.
  - No validator that ticks per day is a multiple of 6.
  - `heading` is not reset on spawn.
  - Determinism and conservation tests never reach a birth.
  - NumPy-major-version dependence.
  - Per-agent Python loops take about 75 % of tick time; about 2.5–3× faster is possible with
    identical results.

**Calibration and statistics** (no calibration change unless refit):
- *Identifiability.* Not identifiable. The phase-B / C doubling times are missed on every seed
  (44.5 vs 55 days; 99 vs 145). The gate's largest-weight target (first litters 104) is
  contested.
- *The guard.* It is a difference test, not an equivalence test:
  - At n = 3, an identical arm reads "not preserved" about 30 % of the time.
  - N@315 at +50 % is caught only about half the time.
  - "Preserved" verdicts, including g = 8, are weak evidence.
  - Fix: TOST equivalence margins, a three-way verdict and ≥ 24 fresh seeds.
- *Seed hygiene.*
  - Evaluation seeds 8000–8002 informed calibration choices.
  - "Out-of-sample" and "never seen" wording is wrong in places.
  - Guard caches are not keyed on config.
- *Forking paths.* Four substrate or rule changes, each triggered by a null on the same 3
  seeds.
- *Landmarks.* Fragile peak day and cessation; N@315 has a CV of about 0.35. Use phase-B
  doubling time instead.

**Learning and measurement**:
- *Criterion 5 is partly mechanical:*
  - Candidate rows include reflex-forced and pup rows.
  - On weaned rows with no reflex, the learned net's locomotor gating drops from +0.82–1.07 to
    +0.09–0.41 (threshold 0.69).
  - H-e "supported" (C varied only through criterion 5) is probably an artefact. This check was
    exploratory, on other seeds and another net.
- *The learner's signal:*
  - One full curiosity relief is worth about 1e-3 in D, against an entropy temperature of
    about 3e-3.
  - Advantages are normalised across decision and sub-step transitions together.
  - λ is applied per transition, not per unit of time.
- *The substrate arc* (v6 → v7 → physics → novelty) is conditioned on nulls. §1.9 even
  describes the form the test reads. A program-level stop rule is needed.
- *Rodent play is mostly social and juvenile.* The substrate has no partner-relative
  primitives and no dyadic trace fields.
- *The v7 null* is not re-gated on contact, so the object bias is about −0.1. Add a
  permutation null of the episode's own actions.
- *Validation.* Synthetic positives are written to the criterion text, so AUC 1.000 shows
  correctness of the code, not validity. A 0 / 18 negative control cannot certify a
  false-positive rate ≤ 10 %; the 95 % upper bound is 15 %.

**Engineering**:
- No provenance: `run.json` lacks commit, config hash, versions and schema.
- Golden hashes are not tracked.
- Config models accept unknown keys.
- Seven near-duplicate reading scripts resume stale runs without checking config.
- `sim.py` has 1,650 lines with one class doing everything.
- Policy kinds are a closed list.
- Pens and species can't be expressed in config (a 3 × 3 pen fails an assert).
- No checkpoint / restore and no CI.
- HANDOFF's architecture map is stale.

## Roadmap (ordered; each tier keeps the NumPy path deterministic and H2 intact)

**Tier 0 — integrity, bit-identical (no calibration change). About 1–2 days.**
- Provenance: `Universe25Sim` writes `config.json` and a manifest into `run.json` (commit,
  dirty flag, config hash, versions, schema versions).
- Track the trajectory-hash tool, plus a golden-hash table across the flag matrix: K = 1,
  K = 12 continue / rechoose, physics, novelty, scarcity, trace on/off, 24 ticks per day.
- `extra="forbid"` on every config model; a validator that ticks per day is a multiple of 6;
  `backend: auto` → NumPy.
- A litters-dropped counter; reset `heading` on spawn (affects only v3+ learned arms).
- A seed ledger; guard caches keyed by config hash; corrected wording.
- Test tiers (fast < 30 s, slow, gpu), a conservation test past births, a `debug_invariants`
  flag; CI with ruff, the fast tier and the H2 audit.

**Tier 1 — measurement and statistics validity (no calibration change). About 2–4 days.**
- Rules v8, preregistered and on a new split (80,000+):
  - criterion 5 counts only rows where a free action was eligible (eligibility recorded in
    the trace);
  - the form test gets two re-simulation nulls re-gated on contact (own-action permutation
    for order; even mix for composition);
  - the locomotor threshold is fixed from the movement model;
  - hard negatives: homeostatic scheduling, area-restricted search, biased i.i.d. mixers,
    instrumental run-ups;
  - ≥ 30 negative-control windows;
  - stop using C as a response.
- Re-read H-e with v8 as exploratory, and report it honestly.
- Guard v2: TOST equivalence margins, three-way verdict, A/A check on fresh seeds 9000+.
- Posterior predictive check of the frozen model (48 seeds, with and without the removal of
  about 150 mice), plus Morris screening of about 41 parameters (about 3.5 h on 6 workers).

**Tier 2 — learner signal and a decisive play experiment (H2-clean). About 2–3 days plus
training.**
- Entropy annealing 0.05 → 0.005, advantages normalised per transition type, λ per unit of
  time.
- Program-level stop rule: no further substrate change until a learner passes a sensitivity
  test.
- **P4-15 consequence sensitivity** (preregistration in the learning review). Does a learner's
  free time depend on object consequences beyond its entropy? The statistic is P(object action
  | near a ball) − P(… | near a fixed object), learned vs random, on 12 evaluation seeds with
  an effect floor of 0.05. If it fails, end substrate changes for PPO and move to H-g / `evo`.

**Tier 3 — replica v2: Calhoun's system, not only his curve (reopens calibration; Jason's
decision). About 2–4 weeks.**
- **Geometry.** 16 wall cells, each with 4 tunnels × 4 stacked boxes and a wall hopper for 25
  mice and 4 bottles for 8. An open centre floor. Physical box occupancy, with choice among
  free boxes.
- **Record configuration without a playground.** The playground becomes the manipulation.
- **Emergent phase A.** 48-day founders, no calendar gate, litter ≈ 5.5, high phase-B
  survival. Targets: first litters at 50–65 days; 620 at day 315; the 55 → 145-day doubling
  break.
- **Social system.**
  - Territories on wall sectors, contested by maturing males; the holder sires.
  - Brood groups and role saturation.
  - Maternal aggression and eviction of young.
  - Withdrawn males (wounded, on the centre floor) split from beautiful ones (unwounded,
    self-grooming).
  - Sex-biased mortality through wounding.
- **Development driven by received care** (nursing deficit, early eviction). Recovery tests
  (Kessler, Marsden transfers) to measure what assuming irreversibility costs.
- **Methods fixes behind flags:** the post-move cell-count cache and synchronous fights.
- **Expanded landmark set:**
  - plateau onset;
  - doubling times;
  - counts 1871 at day 811, 1650 at 960 and 100 at 1471;
  - sex ratios;
  - box vacancy (20 % at 2,200);
  - the removal event;
  - the Fig 4 young-per-group slope.
- **Recalibration.** History matching with an emulator and an ensemble of parameter sets that
  are not ruled out (not a single point). Validate out-of-sample against the 1-, 2-, 4- and
  8-cell sibling universes, which were never fitted.

**Tier 4 — architecture and performance (bit-identical behind the golden hashes). About 1–2
weeks, interleavable.**
- Vectorise the hot loops (about 2.5–3×).
- Split `sim.py` into ordered systems.
- `@register_policy` / `@register_scenario`; `SpeciesParams` / `ActionParams` defaulting to
  today's literals.
- An experiment harness (`ExperimentSpec` → `run_experiment` / `read_arms` / guard) replacing
  the per-card scripts.
- Checkpoint / restore with a round-trip hash test.
- `load_run()` / `collect_runs()` for analysts and the phase 3 dashboard.
- A Warp path with end-to-end parity, or retire it.
